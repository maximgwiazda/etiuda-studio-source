# Icon index

One line per icon: name, its use, and any compromise. 115 icons.

## Chrome

- `menu`: opens the band's menu.
- `settings`: opens Settings. Compromise: eight teeth; a gear thins at 16 px.
- `home`: the home tab (Overview, Library or Team). Compromise: a house; none of its three places' glyphs, so the tab names its kind.
- `maintenance`: the Maintenance tab. Compromise: a wrench, so it stops sharing Settings' gear.
- `search`: the search field.
- `close`: closes a cover, tab or window; takes a card off a card's Next list.
- `tab-close`: a tab's own cross in the band. The desk's drawing (ICON_TAB_X in its icons.js): a 20 grid at a 1.5 stroke, symmetric about the centre so its ink centre is the box's (board 982, finding 17).
- `tab-add`: the band's "+", a new tab. The desk's ICON_TAB_ADD, drawn as `tab-close` is, so the two marks cross on one line.
- `back`: goes back a step; Keyboard shortcuts' Move card left row (the same arrow as `move-up`, turned).
- `forward`: goes forward a step; the Menu's tour row (S1, 2026-10-06); Keyboard shortcuts' Move card right row.
- `chevron-up`: folds a section.
- `chevron-down`: unfolds a section, opens a select, opens the list of open projects.
- `chevron-left`: previous page or pane, scrolls the project tabs back.
- `chevron-right`: next page or pane, scrolls the project tabs on.
- `more`: the overflow menu of a row. Compromise: solid dots.
- `expand`: grows a pane to fill the window.
- `collapse`: shrinks a pane back.
- `drag-handle`: grip for reordering a row. Compromise: solid dots.
- `external-link`: opens outside the app.
- `help`: opens help.
- `info`: a neutral notice; the Menu's About row (S1, 2026-10-06).
- `warning`: a notice that needs care.
- `error`: a notice that something failed.
- `success`: a notice that something landed.
- `loading`: spinner while work runs. Compromise: three quarters of a ring; the app spins it.
## Theme

- `theme-light`: switch to the light theme.
- `theme-dark`: switch to the dark theme.
- `theme-system`: follow the system theme, Settings only. Compromise: a half-filled disc rather than sun and moon in one.
## Project

- `new-project`: New project.
- `open-project`: Open a folder. Compromise: flap corners are stroke joins, not the set's radius.
- `rename`: rename a project, tab or tag. Compromise: a text cursor; no pencil, that is edit.
- `delete`: Delete, Delete card.
- `folder`: a folder on disk.
- `file`: a file on disk.
- `catalog`: the catalog itself. Compromise: a closed book.
- `archive`: the Archive, Put away.
- `restore`: bring an edition or project back.
- `history`: revisions over time.
- `places`: the open projects side by side, left of the tabs (board 862). Compromise: four places, the one you are in filled.
## Authoring

- `cards`: the Cards section.
- `card`: one reply card.
- `intents`: the Intents section.
- `intent`: one intent clause.
- `facts`: Quick facts. Compromise: a bulleted list.
- `tags`: tags of any kind.
- `categories`: the Categories section. Compromise: two items on a shelf.
- `edit`: edit a card or tag.
- `copy`: copy to the clipboard.
- `paste`: paste from the clipboard.
- `cut`: cut to the clipboard.
- `undo`: undo the last edit.
- `redo`: redo an undone edit.
- `duplicate`: Duplicate a card.
- `move-up`: move a row up; Keyboard shortcuts' Move next card up row.
- `move-down`: move a row down; Keyboard shortcuts' Move next card down row.
- `add`: New card, intent, category.
- `remove`: take one item out of a list.
- `filter`: narrow a list.
- `sort`: change a list's order.
- `pin`: keep a card at the top.
- `favourite`: mark a card as a favourite. Compromise: solid.
- `language-en`: the English column or toggle. Compromise: letters drawn as strokes, not text.
- `language-pl`: the Polish column or toggle. Compromise: letters drawn as strokes, not text.
- `translate`: write the other language. Compromise: an A with two arrows.
- `placeholder`: a {slot} in a body. Compromise: braces round a dot.
- `alternative`: one of several wordings. Compromise: a fork.
- `note`: a note on a card or edition.
- `commitment`: the mark on a card that commits the firm. Compromise: a rubber stamp; at 16 px a seal read as a target, a cog or the licence glyph.
- `paths`: the Paths map, the cards that follow one another. Compromise: one card forking to two.
## Import and review

- `import`: Import a file, Import catalog.
- `export`: Export an edition.
- `csv`: a CSV file. Compromise: a table, no letters.
- `review`: review a proposal.
- `proposal`: a file an agent dropped in. Compromise: a page with an arrow entering.
- `accept`: Accept a change.
- `reject`: Reject a change. Compromise: a barred circle, since close owns the cross.
- `diff`: What changes, was and becomes.
- `merge`: Land the accepted changes.
- `awaiting`: second language not yet written. Compromise: an empty card with an ellipsis.
- `lint`: the Lint section; Keyboard shortcuts' Next finding row (a finding is a row of Checks).
- `lint-warning`: a lint warning row.
- `lint-error`: a lint error row.
## Publish and channel

- `publish`: Publish rev N.
- `edition`: one published edition. Compromise: a page with a bookmark.
- `desk`: one desk that reads the share.
- `desks`: the desks together.
- `statistics`: the Statistics section.
- `request`: Ask for statistics. Compromise: an envelope.
- `sync`: two-way sync with the share.
- `refresh`: read the folder again.
- `period`: the span a count covers. Compromise: no arrow heads, too small at 16 px.
- `calendar`: a date.
- `clock`: a time.
- `download`: save a copy to disk.
- `upload`: send a file up.
- `eject`: unload the catalog.
- `load`: load a catalog.
- `replace`: Replace catalog?, Find and replace. Compromise: a two-arrow loop.
## Trust

- `lock`: locked, read only.
- `unlock`: unlocked.
- `key`: a key or secret.
- `backup`: a backup copy of the signing key, the corner's disc and the key row. Glyph C, the key inside a turn, drawn at 20 px with its line at 1.4 (Maxim 2026-10-07 21:38 and 22:18).
- `licence`: the desk licence. Compromise: a rosette.
- `signed`: a signed edition. Compromise: a page with a signature.
- `verified`: a verified signature.
- `user`: a person.
## Desk

- `change`: the Change... button. Compromise: two opposed arrows.
- `chevron-retreat`: hides the overflowing buttons.
- `keyboard`: keyboard settings.
- `shortcut`: a keyboard shortcut. Compromise: a key cap with a bolt.
- `tab`: a project tab; Keyboard shortcuts' Next tab and Previous tab rows (the designer's, 2026-11-16).
- `zoom-in`: Keyboard shortcuts' Zoom in row. `search` with a plus inside its lens, so the three zooms read as one family.
- `zoom-out`: Keyboard shortcuts' Zoom out row. `search` with a minus inside its lens.
- `zoom-reset`: Keyboard shortcuts' Reset zoom row. `search` with an equals sign inside its lens.
